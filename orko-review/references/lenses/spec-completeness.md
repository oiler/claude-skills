## Lens: completeness

Read the spec as the person who has to write the plan from it, with nothing else to go on:

- Terms used without definition, or used with two meanings.
- Requirements with two plausible readings. Name both readings.
- Requirements nobody could test. Say what observable behavior would make them testable.
- Missing behavior: errors, empty inputs, edge cases, concurrency, what happens on a second run.
- Missing or vague non-goals, where a planner could reasonably build more than intended.
- Decisions deferred without saying who makes them or when.
