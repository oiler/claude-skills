## Lens: accuracy

Check every factual claim the document makes against its source:

- Claims about the codebase, the project, and its history. Open the file or check git.
- Claims about platforms, tools, products, and versions. Check the docs the document cites, or the installed source.
- Numbers, dates, and survey claims. Recompute or rerun them where you can.
- Quotes of other documents: compare them word for word.
