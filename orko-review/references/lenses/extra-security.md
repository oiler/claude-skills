## Lens: security

Invoke the web-security skill before you start. Then check what the document proposes for:

- Authentication, authorization, and session handling.
- Handling of untrusted input, and encoding of output.
- Secrets and credentials: where they live, who can read them, what gets logged.
- Supply chain: new dependencies, pinned versions, install-time scripts.
- Anything that runs commands, writes files, or makes network calls on the user's behalf.
