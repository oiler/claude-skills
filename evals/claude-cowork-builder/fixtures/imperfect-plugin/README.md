# Team Ops

Team Ops is a small pair of tools for the weekly rhythm of a team: writing the Friday status report, and keeping the CRM tidy enough that the report is worth reading.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| /weekly-report | Builds the week's status report — what shipped, what slipped, what is next — and saves it as a page you can share. |
| /crm-sync | Looks through your CRM for records missing an owner or a next step, and for the same company entered twice, then fixes the ones you approve. |

## Getting started

Ask for the weekly report and paste in whatever the team wrote down during the week — standup notes, a doc, messages copied out of chat. Nothing has to be connected for the report itself.

The CRM tidy-up talks to your CRM directly, so that one needs your CRM connected before it can do anything.

## Settings

None required.
